## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (confirmed by the owner). Deployable to GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel as-is.

## Users

People from the Jardim Vergueiro area (zona sul de São Paulo) and nearby who are hungry now, mostly on a phone, often arriving from Instagram or Google Maps. They want to see the burgers, decide, and either order delivery or go to the house.

## Product Purpose

Bacon Burguer is a burger house ("hamburgueria") in São Paulo. The website is a showcase ("site vitrine"): no ordering system of its own. Its job is to make people want the food and the place, then send them to order on Saipos (the house's own delivery/ordering page) or Keeta, or to visit. Success = taps on "Pedir" links and visits.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood burger house with a periferia identity: pretzel-style dark artesanal buns, bacon-forward burgers, and a big warehouse-like space with a bookshelf, pop-culture portraits, plants, a sinuca (pool) table and a mezzanine. Black, red and white with the pig logo.
